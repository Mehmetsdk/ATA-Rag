Tuition and Fees

Tuition varies by programme and study mode.

Computer Science tuition

Full-time Computer Science tuition at the Faculty of Computer Science is 6,500 PLN per academic year for domestic students and 2,200 EUR per year for international students.

Part-time studies

Part-time (weekend) programmes cost approximately 4,800 PLN per year, payable in two installments.

Scholarships

Students with excellent academic results may apply for a rector's scholarship covering up to 50% of tuition. Applications open each September.
