Admissions

The admission period for the 2026/2027 academic year begins on July 1, 2026 and ends on September 20, 2026.

Required documents

  • High school diploma
  • Passport or ID card copy
  • Application form
  • Proof of payment of the recruitment fee

How to apply

Complete the online application form, upload the required documents, and follow the programme-specific steps listed on the admissions pages. Deadlines vary by programme and intake.

Computer Science admission requirements

Applicants to the Computer Science programme at the Faculty of Computer Science must submit a matura certificate with a passing grade in mathematics.

Contact the admissions office for more information.
